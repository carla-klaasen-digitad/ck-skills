# Oikos — Canada On-Site Content Profile

Market: Canada (bilingual EN/FR — Canadian English + Québec French). Source: Danone Canada SEO Content Rules doc.

For general Canada-wide legal/vocabulary/language conventions that apply to every brand, see `general_legal_canada.md` — this file covers only what's specific to Oikos Canada.

---

## Brand Rules

- Do not target the keyword "full fat greek yogurt"
- Oikos' "30% less sugar" claim does not qualify for a separate "low sugar" claim — never combine or imply both

## Vocabulary

- "natural" → not permitted, including for organic products — if organic, always say "organic" instead of "natural"
- "a good source of potassium" → not permitted, do not mention
- "essential" (referring to products) → not permitted
- "high-protein" → use "With high protein" instead (no hyphen)

Note: this is narrower in scope than the US Oikos on-site profile (`oikos.md`) — no Canada-specific content-brief, FAQ, or product-page-structure documentation has been found yet. Follow `general_legal_canada.md` for everything else.

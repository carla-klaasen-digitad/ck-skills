## Brand Rules
- Never claim "no artificial sweeteners" broadly — this only applies to the Too Good & Co. REMIX sub-line, not Light+Fit REMIX (which contains sucralose and acesulfame potassium)
- Never claim gluten-free or nut-free — real allergens are present in specific sub-lines

## Vocabulary
- healthy → not permitted
- the best / #1 → not permitted
- natural → not permitted
- guilt-free → not permitted
- probiotic → not permitted; use "live and active cultures"
- sugar-free → not permitted; use "no added sugar" only where the specific product qualifies
- clean label, wholesome, nutritious → not permitted

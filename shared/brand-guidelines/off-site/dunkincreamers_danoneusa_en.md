## Brand Rules
- Never imply dairy is health-forward — lead with taste and ritual only, if the category is discussed at all
- Never make health claims or disease prevention claims
- Never claim "plant-based" — this is a dairy creamer, it does not qualify

## Vocabulary
- healthy → not permitted
- the best / #1 → not permitted without a sourced, current award citation
- natural → not permitted (FDA-undefined)
- guilt-free → not permitted
- made in US → not permitted; use "prepared in the US"
- clean label → not permitted until the ingredient-list renovation is confirmed complete
- Follow all restrictions in general_danoneusa.md

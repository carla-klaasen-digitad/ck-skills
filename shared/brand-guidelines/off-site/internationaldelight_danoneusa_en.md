## Brand Rules
- Never associate the product with "health" claims
- Never make unverified environmental claims
- Never claim medical or physiological benefits
- Do not denigrate competitors (Coffee Mate, Starbucks, Chobani)

## Vocabulary
- healthy, wholesome, wellness, essential → not permitted
- guilt-free, treat, satiety → not permitted
- low sugar (standalone) → not permitted
- natural → not permitted; use "naturally sourced"
- additives → not permitted; use "preservatives"
- fresh fruits → not permitted
- made in US → not permitted; use "prepared in the US"
- Follow all restrictions in general_danoneusa.md

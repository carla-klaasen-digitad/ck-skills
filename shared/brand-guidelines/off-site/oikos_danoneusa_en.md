## Brand Rules
- Never call OIKOS PRO "yogurt" — use "snack" or "dairy snack" if ever referenced generically
- Never say "0g sugar" or "zero sugar" — only "0g added sugar"
- Do not compare Oikos to other yogurt brands

## Vocabulary
- healthy, wholesome → not permitted; use "nutritious"
- benefits, wellness, essential → not permitted
- low sugar (standalone) → not permitted; state exact grams per portion instead
- treat, guilt-free, satiety → not permitted
- natural, fresh fruits → not permitted; use "naturally sourced"
- additives → not permitted; use "preservatives"
- lactoseless → not permitted; use "lactose free"
- Follow all restrictions in general_danoneusa.md

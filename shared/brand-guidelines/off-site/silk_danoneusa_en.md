## Brand Rules
- Never make disease claims (e.g. "reduces risk of heart disease," "lowers cholesterol")
- Never claim a product "builds" or "repairs" muscle without a clinical disclaimer
- Never claim a product "boosts immunity"
- Never name a competitor in a comparison without substantiation data and client approval
- Never describe a product with added sugar as "sweetened" — use flavor descriptors instead ("Vanilla," "Chocolate")

## Vocabulary
- natural → not permitted (FDA has not defined "natural" for food labeling)
- healthy → not permitted unless the specific product meets FDA fat/sodium/nutrient criteria
- high protein / good source of protein → use with caution — must match the FDA %DV threshold for the specific product; do not default to this claim
- Follow all restrictions in general_danoneusa.md

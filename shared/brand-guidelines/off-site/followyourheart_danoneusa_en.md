## Brand Rules
- Never make disease prevention or treatment claims
- Never claim any product is "nut-free" (coconut cross-contamination risk exists)

## Vocabulary
- healthy → not permitted
- the best → not permitted
- natural → not permitted; use "naturally sourced" or specific ingredient descriptions
- guilt-free → not permitted
- clean (as in "clean eating") → not permitted, too vague
- dairy alternative → not permitted; use "dairy-free"
- Follow all restrictions in general_danoneusa.md

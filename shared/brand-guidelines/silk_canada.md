# Silk — Canada On-Site Content Profile

Market: Canada (bilingual EN/FR — Canadian English + Québec French). Source: Danone Canada SEO Content Rules doc.

For general Canada-wide legal/vocabulary/language conventions that apply to every brand, see `general_legal_canada.md` — this file covers only what's specific to Silk.

---

## Brand Rules

- Do not disparage dairy or lactose-free products — stay neutral, or favour the flexitarian stance
- Sustainability: be cautious — the commissioned environmental study shows plant-based beverages are worse than Canadian milk for water consumption and water scarcity. Do not over-claim on the "green" side of plant-based beverages.
- "Nextmilk" — do not capitalize "milk" within this product name

## Vocabulary

- "rich" (in nutrients, calcium, etc.) → not permitted; use "contains" or "source of…"
- "milk" / "cream" → not permitted for the beverage itself; use "almond base," "almond-based beverage," or "plant-based beverage" (e.g. never "almond milk") — tolerated in meta-titles/metadata only
- "creamer" → acceptable (for coffee whiteners specifically)
- "plant-based yogurt" as a product name → not permitted; use "style yogurt" / "Style Yogourt Silk" (French: "MD" superscript)
- "healthy food" → not permitted; use "healthy lifestyle" / "healthy way of life" only
- "Non-GMO Project verified" → prefer "free of GMOs" or "GMO-free"
- "high protein" → never hyphenated
- "drink yogurt" → use "drinkable yogurt" (French: « le yogourt à boire »)
- "additives" → use "preservatives"
- "treat" → not permitted
- FR: for high-protein dishes use « protéinés », « source de protéines », or « à teneur élevée en protéines » — never « riches en protéines »
- FR: recipes using plant-based yogurt must state « yogourt végétal » / « yogourt à base de plantes » and "plant-based yogurt" in English
- FR product naming: write « boisson Silk Protéinée 1,42 L régulière » — not « …1,42 L régulière nature »
- FR flavour phrasing example: « Le yogourt à boire à saveur de baies mélangées »

**Words to avoid for Silk specifically:** milk, cream, « yogourt végétal » (as a standalone unqualified term), healthy food, sustainability (unqualified claims)
